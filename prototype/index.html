About us

The Greer Soup Kitchen, run by Daily Bread Ministries, is a non-profit in Greer, South Carolina. Local citizens started it in 1990. We receive no government or United Way funding. We rely on gifts from local people, businesses, and churches.

Greer Soup Kitchen

Open 365 days a year. We serve over 150 free, hot, healthy meals every day in a restaurant-style dining room. Everyone is welcome. No form is needed for a meal.

Greer STEP

Shelter to Empower People. A two-year program that gives homeless families a place to live and support with jobs, savings, and finding a home of their own.
